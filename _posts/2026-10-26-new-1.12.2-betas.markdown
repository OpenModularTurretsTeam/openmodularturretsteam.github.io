Hello everyone,
<br>
new betas for 1.12.2 are out: **OMT, OMLib and OMPD 4.0.0-beta.2** and **Open Modular Lighting 1.0.0-beta.7**.
All of them need OMLib 4.0.0-beta.2 or newer.

The 1.12.2 versions catch up with 1.20.1+:

- **Claiming**: right-click a turret base, turret, camouflage trap or hardened block/wall/fence without an owner to claim it, together with the unowned blocks connected to it; unowned blocks sparkle green
- **Ownership Wand** for mapmakers (creative, operators only), and turrets can't hurt you while you hold it
- **Disown** your turret bases and camouflage traps
- **Safer bases**: only admins can attach turrets, hoppers and pipes can no longer take the ammo out, and bases without an owner stay inert
- **Placement outline** shows why a turret can't be placed
- The server sends its **config** to the clients, so tooltips and the placement outline follow the server's rules
- Hardened blocks can be used as **camouflage**
- **OML**: floodlights are disguised in OMLib's camouflage menu, like turret bases and camouflage traps; existing disguises are kept
- Lots of fixes, among them ComputerCraft/OpenComputers access levels and messages that could load far-away chunks

The 1.20.1+ versions are still OMT 4.0.0-beta.9 and OML 1.0.0-beta.5.

The full changelogs are here: [OMT 4.0.0](/changelog/4.0.0/) and [OML 1.0.0](/changelog/oml-1.0.0/).
