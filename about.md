This is the page for the mods of the open modular mod suite.

You can find the source code for our mods at GitHub:  
[OpenModularTurrets](https://github.com/OpenModularTurretsTeam/OpenModularTurrets) (branch `1.20.1+` for 1.20.1 and 1.21.1, branch `1.12` for 1.12.2)  
[OMLib](https://github.com/OpenModularTurretsTeam/OMLib) (1.12.2 only)  
[OpenModularPassiveDefense](https://github.com/OpenModularTurretsTeam/OpenModularPassiveDefense) (1.12.2 only, part of OpenModularTurrets on 1.20.1 and 1.21.1)  
[OpenModularLighting](https://github.com/OpenModularTurretsTeam/OpenModularLighting) (branch `1.20.1+` for 1.20.1, 1.21.1 and 1.21.11, branch `1.12` for 1.12.2)  
[OMTControl](https://github.com/OpenModularTurretsTeam/OMTControl) (discontinued)  

See the [full changelog of Open Modular Turrets 4.0.0](/changelog/4.0.0/) and the [full changelog of Open Modular Lighting 1.0.0](/changelog/oml-1.0.0/).
