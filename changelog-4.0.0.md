## What you need

- **1.12.2:** Open Modular Turrets, Open Modular Passive Defense and Open Modular Lighting each require OMLib of the same pre-release or newer.
- **1.20.1, 1.21.1 and 1.21.11:** Open Modular Turrets and Open Modular Lighting are all you need: Open Modular Passive Defense's blocks are part of Open Modular Turrets, and both mods bundle OMLib.

The changelog of Open Modular Lighting covers both Minecraft versions on one page, with the changes of one version marked.
